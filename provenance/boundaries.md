## The boundary between juan 19 and 20

The site presents 易學啓蒙 as a parent page with five children rather than two
juan-level pages. The [opening facsimile linked from 明蓍策第三](https://www.eee-learning.com/eeebook/middleyi/1998.png)
visibly reads 御纂周易折中卷二十 and 啓蒙下, followed by 明蓍策第三. This establishes the
split after 原卦畫第二. The same leaf identifies the facsimile as 欽定四庫全書薈要.
This page was visually inspected; no OCR or inferred text was inserted into the corpus.

The front-matter order follows 易學網's own book navigation, rather than being
reordered to match another edition. The formal 卷首 remains separate from that material.
